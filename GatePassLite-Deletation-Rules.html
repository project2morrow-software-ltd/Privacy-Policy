Delete Account – Gate Pass Lite

Users may request deletion of their Gate Pass Lite account and associated data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request account deletion, please send an email to:

info@p2msoft.com

Email Subject:
Delete My Gate Pass Lite Account

Please include the following information:

  • Registered email address
  • Organization name
  • Device ID (optional)

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon successful verification, we may delete:

  • User account information
  • Attendance records
  • Face recognition data
  • Employee profile information
  • Device registration information

Data Retention

Certain data may be retained temporarily if required for:

  • Legal obligations
  • Security investigations
  • Enterprise audit requirements

Google Drive Backups

If users enabled personal Google Drive backup, backup files stored in the user’s own Google Drive account are controlled entirely by the user.

Users may manually delete backup files directly from their Google Drive account.

Project 2morrow Software Limited does not have access to users’ private Google Drive backup files.
