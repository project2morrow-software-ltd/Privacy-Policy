# Privacy Policy for Gate Pass Lite

**Effective Date:** May 13, 2026

Project 2morrow Software Limited ("we", "us", or "our") operates the **Gate Pass Lite** mobile application (the "Application"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your information when you use our Application.

---

# 1. Information Collection and Use

To provide attendance management, employee verification, and gate pass services, we may collect the following types of information.

## Personal Identification Information

While using the Application, we may collect:

* Full Name
* Official Email Address
* Employee ID / Employee Code
* Department
* Designation
* Profile Picture and Face Data

## Device Identifiers

We may collect device-specific identifiers such as the **Android ID** solely for:

* Secure device-based license activation
* Device authentication and validation
* Preventing unauthorized access
* Enterprise security and account protection

The Android ID is not used for advertising, profiling, or cross-app user tracking.

## Usage Data

We may collect technical and usage-related information about how the Application is accessed and used for troubleshooting, analytics, performance monitoring, and service improvement.

---

# 2. Device Permissions and Their Purpose

The Application requires certain permissions to provide its core functionality.

## Camera Permission (`CAMERA`)

Used exclusively for:

* AI-based face recognition
* Attendance verification
* User identity authentication

The camera is not used for advertising or unauthorized recording.

## Network & Internet Permissions

### Internet Access (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`)

Used for:

* Synchronizing attendance and gate pass records
* Secure communication with cloud servers
* License validation and authentication

### Wi-Fi Discovery (`ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`, `CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE`)

Used to:

* Discover and communicate with authorized local network hardware
* Connect with supported electronic gate controllers and enterprise devices

# 3. Face Data Processing and Security

Face data is collected solely for employee identity verification, attendance authentication, and enterprise security purposes.

We do not:

* Sell face data
* Share face data with advertisers
* Use face data for marketing or profiling purposes

Face data is processed securely using industry-standard protection mechanisms and is accessible only to authorized systems and administrators.

Face data is retained only for as long as required for attendance verification and organizational security purposes.

Users may request deletion of their face data through their organization administrator or by contacting us directly.
